Show 
$$
E \left[ \!\left[ M_{n+k} | Y_{1} , \dots , Y_{n} \right]\! \right] = M_{n}.
$$
Also show that $E[M_{\tau}]=E[M_{1}]$.
- $M_{n}$ is a martingale if
	- It is integrable
	- It is a function of observables
	- martingale condition
- If some $M_{n}$ satisfies the first 2 condition, an for all bounded stop times, $E[M_{\tau}]=E[M_{1}]$, then we get a martingale.
