# All Proper series are locally finite (weighted automata)
---
Consider the proper series $(f^i)_{i\in \mathbb{N}}$ where we define:
- $f^0= \mathbb 1$
- $f^1=f$
- $f^{l+1}=f^l f$

$f$ is proper if $<f, \epsilon\geq 0$ and for all proper series $f^i$ is locally finite and hence always summable.
