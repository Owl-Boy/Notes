# Discrete Homotopy Theory
---
## About
Discrete homotopy theory is an emerging area of mathematics concerned with using homotopy-theoretic techniques to study combinatorial properties of (simple) graphs. It does so by introducing a new combinatorial notion of homotopy between graph maps and subsequently reinterpreting the usual homotopy-theoretic invariants, such as homotopy or homology groups, through the lenses of this new notion.
