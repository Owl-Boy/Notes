# Topos Theory

## About

**Topos Theory** come up independently in 2 different situations.
- In Category theory, by Lawvere
- In Algebraic Geometry, by Grothendiek

In 1963, Cohen gave is proof for the independence of CH, by coming up with the technique of forcing. Scott-Solvay generalized it to the idea of boolean valued models, and the idea of generalizing it to Heyting valued models came up naturally.

Meanwhile Lawvere developed the idea of elementary topoi to capture the axioms of set theory in a category theoretic context.

These 2 ideas together led to the development of Topoi which later also encompassed all Grothendiek sheaf categories
