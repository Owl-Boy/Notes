# Ring Theory
---
**Rings** (and modules) are defined by *decorating* [[Abelian Groups]] with additional data. 
As motivation for the introduction of such structures, note that all number-based examples of groups that we have encountered, such as $\mathbb{Z}$ or $\mathbb{R}$, are endowed with an operation of multiplication as well as the ‘addition’ making them into (abelian) groups.
