# Equivalence of more complicated structures - Semi-groups
---
Consider semi groups as defined in [[Semi Groups in Types Theory]]. Let $A$ be a semigroup and $B$ be another type. Loosely speaking, one can make the claim that a bijection between $2$ sets implies induces an isomorphic structure form one to another.
