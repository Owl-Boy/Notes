# Category of Abelian Groups is closed
---
Consider the hom bifunctor
$$
\text{Ab}^\text{op}\times\text{Ab}\xrightarrow{\text{Hom}} \text{Ab}
$$
Fixing the contravariant variable, there is an adjunction
![[Pasted image 20250708122246.png|400]]
which defines the tensor product, we can now use [[Adjunction of Bifunctors]] to extend this to the tensort product bifunctor 
$$
\text{Ab}\times \text{Ab} \xrightarrow{\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}} \text{Ab}
$$
such that the tensort product and hom define a [[Two Variable Adjunction]].
