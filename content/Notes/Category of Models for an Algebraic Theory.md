# Category of Models for an Algebraic Theory
---
>[!definition]
>A category $A$ is called a **category of models for an algebraic theory** if there is a [[Finitary Functors|finitary]] [[Monadic Functors|monadic]] functor $U:A\to\text{Set}$.

Any category of models for an algebraic theory is finitely presentable, as in the definition of [[locally presentable categories]]
