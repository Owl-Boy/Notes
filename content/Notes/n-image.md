# $n$-image
---
>[!definition]
>Let $f:A\to B$ be a function, then the $n$-image of $f$ is defined as follows:
>$$
>\text{im}_{n}(f):\equiv \sum_{b:B}\big\|\text{fib}_{f}(b)\|_{n}
>$$
