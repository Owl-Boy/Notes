# Some Algorithms for finding the Minimum Spanning Trees
---
Here are a bunch of Algorithms for finding the [[Minimum Spanning Trees|MST]] of a given graph.

- [[Boruvka's Algorithm|Boruvka]]: $O(|E| \log|V|)$
- Jarnik, [[Prim's Algorithm|Prim]] and Dijkstra (independently): $O((|V|+|E|)\log|V|)$
- [[Kruskal's Algorithm| Kruskal]]: $O(|E|+|V| \log |V|)$
- Yao: $O(|E| \log \log |V|)$
- [[Fredmen and Tarjan's Algorithm| Fredman, Tarjan]]: $O(|E| \log^* |V|)$
- [[Karger, Klein and Tarjan's Algorithm|Karger, Klein, Tarjan]]: $O(E)$ randomized
- Pettie, Ramachandran: Optimal but no known running time.
