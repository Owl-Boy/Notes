#  Examples of Pushouts
---
>[!example]
>The figure eight, or the wedge of two circles $S_{1} \vee S_{1}$ can be defined as the pushout diagram of $S_{1} \leftarrow * \rightarrow S_{1}$ in the category of unbased spaces or as the coproduct of $S_{1}$ with itself in the category of based spaces.
