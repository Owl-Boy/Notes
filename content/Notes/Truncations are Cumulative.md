# Truncations are Cumulative
---
>[!lemma]
>Let $k, n \geq -2$ with $k<n$ and $A:\cal U$. Then $\|\|A\|_{n}\|_{k}=\|A\|_{k}$.

We define 2 maps $f:\|\|A\|_{n}\|_{k}\to\|A\|_{k}$ defined as
$$
f(||a|_{n}|_{k}):\equiv|a|_{k}
$$
and we define $g:\|A\|_{k}\to\|\|A\|_{n}\|_{k}$ as
$$
g(|a|_{k}):\equiv ||a|_{n}|_{k}
$$

The map $f$ is well defined as $\|A\|_{k}$ is $k$ truncated so also $n$ truncated. And the map $g$ is well defined because $\|\|A\|_{n}\|_{k}$ is $k$ truncated.
