# Adjoint Functor Theorem for locally presentable categories
---
>[!theorem]
>A functor $F:C\to D$ between [[locally presentable categories]]
>- admits a right adjoint iff it is cocontinuous
>- admits a left adjoint iff it is continuous and accessible

[[General Adjoint Functor Theorem]] states that any accessible functor satisfies the solution set condition.

The [[Special Adjunct Functor Theorem]] proves that colimit generating set of objects $S$ defines a separator for $C$. And fsr any object in a locally presentable category admits at most a sets worth of epimorphic quotients, up to isomorphism. Cocompleteness then implies that cointersections of quotient objects always exist.
