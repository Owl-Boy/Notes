#  Epimorphisms and Monomorphisms Exact Sequences
---
***Monomorphism***, The following exact sequence forces $\alpha$ to be a monomorphism.
$$
\cdots \longrightarrow 0 \longrightarrow L \xrightarrow{\;\alpha\;} M \longrightarrow \cdots
$$

***Epimorphism***, The following exact sequence forces $\beta$ to be an epimorphism.
$$
\cdots \longrightarrow L \xrightarrow{\;\beta\;} M \longrightarrow 0\longrightarrow\cdots
$$
