#  Product of topoi is a topos

---

Given any 2 categories, one can take their product and perform pair-wise constructions. Thus product of categories with finite limits will have all finite limits (take the limits component wise).

The exponential objects can be defined pairwise and evaluated pairwise.
$$
\langle C, D\rangle^{\langle A, B\rangle} \times \langle A, B\rangle = \langle C^A \times A, D^B \times B \rangle
$$

And the sub-object classifiers can also be defined component wise in a manner than is no different. Hence product of any 2 topoi is a topos.
