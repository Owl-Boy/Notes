#  Finset is a topos

Finite products and coproducts of finite sets are finite, thus, by [[Set is Complete and Cocomplete]], we get that Finset has finite limits and finite colimits. Note that Finset is **not** complete or cocomplete as finite product and coproducts don't exist.

Given sets $A$ and $B$, the hom set $A^B$ has size $|A|^|B|$ and is this, also finite.

Finset has the same sub-object classifier as set.
