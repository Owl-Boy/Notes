# Cartesian Closed Category
---
>[!definition]
>A category $C$ with finite products is called **Catesian Closed** or a **CCC**, if it has a the structure of [[Monoidal Category]] using its product, and with the internal hom functor, forms the structure of a [[Monoidal Closed Category]].

>[!example]
>$\text{Set}$ is CCC.
>$R$-mod is CCC.
>$\text{Cat}$ is CCC.
>Category of compactly generated hausdorf spaces is CCC.
