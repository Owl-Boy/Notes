#  Limits and Colimits as Kan Extensions
---
Consider a category $J$ and a diagram $F:J\to C$, then the [[Kan Extensions]] of $F$ along the functor $J\to \mathbb 1$ define the limit and colimit of the diagram $F$. 

The colimit/limit exists iff the kan extension exists.
