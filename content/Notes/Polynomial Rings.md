# Polynomial Rings
---
>[!definition]
>Given a ring $R$, consider the polynomials in the variable $x$ whose coefficients in $R$. The set of these polynomials define a ring.
>
>The notation for such rings are $R[x]$
>
>The additive unit and multiplicative units of the ring are $0$ and $1$ from $R$ respectively.

There is also a ring of power-series, which is similar which is denoted as $R[[x]]$.
