# PSPACE
---
>[!definition]
>The collection of problems such that the amount of cells used in the work tape are bounded by some polynomial $p$ in terms of the size of the input are called $\text{P-SPACE}$

^92ad13

Some known problems in $\text{P-SPACE}$ are:
- [[Quantified Boolean Formulas]]
- [[Complexity of FO|Model Checking of FO]]
- Determining if a regular expression matches a string
- [[Type Inhabitation in P-SPACE]]
