# Isomorphism Types of Models
---
Given the equivalence relation $\cong$ on the class of finite $\sigma_n$-structures $\text{STRUCT}[\sigma_{n}]$, and isomorphism types means an equivalence class.

Let $\tau$ be used to denote isomorphism types. Given an $\vec{a}\in A^n$, we say that $\vec{a}$ $d$-realizes $\tau$ in $\mathfrak A$ if $N_{d}^\mathfrak A(\vec{a})$ is of type $\tau$. (we omit the $d$ and $\mathfrak A$ if it is understood from the context.)

>[!lemma]
>Let $\mathfrak {A, B} \in \text{STRUCT}[\sigma]$. Then $\mathfrak A\leftrightarrows_{d} \mathfrak B$ iff for each isomorphism type $\tau$ of $\sigma_{1}$ structures, the number of elements of $\frak A$ and $\frak B$ that $d$-realize $\tau$ are the same.
