#  Forgetful Functors that do not have an adjoint
---
The functors:
- $\text{Field}\xrightarrow{U}\text{Ring}$
- $\text{Field}\xrightarrow{U}\text{Ab}$
- $\text{Field}\xrightarrow{U}\text{Set}$
- $\text{Field}\xrightarrow{(-)^\times}\text{Ab}$
Forget algebraic structure but do not admit left or right adjoints.

To see that no adjoint is possible, note that there exists a map in the target category from $\mathbb{Z}$ to fields of any characterisitc. But this is not possible as there are no non-trivial maps between fields of 2 different characterisitcs.
