#  Top is Complete and Cocomplete
---
The forgotful functor $U:\text{Top} \to \text{Set}$ is represented by the singleton space $*$. So [[Representable Universal Property of Colimits]] says that all colimits in $\text{Top}$ can be constructed by adding a topology to colimits of $\text{Set}$, which was discussed for products in [[Products in Topology from Products in Category Theory]].

To define an equalizer of the diagram $f, g:X \rightrightarrows Y$, one can consider teh set equalizer and give it the sub-space topology. Which the coarsest topology that makes the equalizer $E$ have a continuous inclusion into $X$.

Dually we get that colimits can also be found in this manner, we first find the colimjt of the underying set and then give it a topology. 

Coproducts are formed by assigning the disjoint union the finest topology that makes the inclusion maps continuous.

The Coequalizer, we get the coequalizer set $Y / \sim$ where the equivalence relation $\sim$  is generated by $f(x)\sim g(x)$, and the we give it the finest topology so that the quotient map is continuous.

And since [[Any Category with Coproducts and Coequalizers is Cocomplete, with Products and Equalizers is Complete]], $\text{Top}$ is complete and co-complete.
