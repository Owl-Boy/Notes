# A natural isomorphism between diagrams induces an isomorphism between limits and colimits
---
>[!theorem]
>A natural isomorphism between diagrams induces a naturally-defined isomorphism between their limits or colimits, whenever these exist. 

We already have [[Choosing Limits of diagrams in Functorial]], so we have a functor $C^J\to C$ for picking limits, and like all functors, isomorphisms go to isomorphisms. :3
