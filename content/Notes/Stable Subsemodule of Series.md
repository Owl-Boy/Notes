# Stable Sub-semimodule of a Series 
---
Given a [[Weighted Automata on Series|series]] defined as $\mathbb S\langle\!\langle \Sigma^* \rangle\!\rangle$ this can be thought of as a module we quotient a series $S\in \mathbb S\langle\!\langle\Sigma^*\rangle\!\rangle$ with a polynomial $p$ we can write $S=p \cdot S'$ then quotienting $S$ by $p$ gives $S'$. Any series that can't be broken down then it dies. So we define the quotient $B$ of $A \subseteq \mathbb S\langle\!\langle\Sigma^*\rangle\!\rangle$ with $p$ as:
$$
p^{-1}A = B = \{  S' | p\cdot S' \in A \}
$$

>[!definition]
>We say that a subset $A$ is stable if $p^{-1}A \subseteq A$.
