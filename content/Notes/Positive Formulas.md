# Positive Formulas
---
Since monotonicity is undecidable, to construct a logic with least fixed points, one can consider a smaller class of formulas that are guaranteed to give a monotone operator.

>[!definition]
>Given a formula $\varphi$ we say that an instance of a relation $R$ is negative if it is in the scope of an odd number of negations, otherwise it is called positive.
>
>We say that the formula is positive if all instances of $R$ are positive.

>[!theorem]
>If a formula $\varphi$ is positive, then $F_{\varphi}$ is monotone.

The above apparently has an easy inductive proof on the structure of the formula.
