# Slice Wise Polynomial Time (XP)
---
>[!definition]
>This is the class of decision problems that accept a [[Parameterized Languages|parameterized language]] $P$ such that there exists an algorithm:
>- which, for every natural number $n$, the language $(\text{fst}\ P, n)$ can be decided in polynomial time.
