# Canonical Presentations using Free Algebras
---
>[!theorem]
>Let $(T, \eta, \mu)$ be a monad on $C$ and let $(A, TA \xrightarrow \alpha A)$ be a $T$-algebra, then
>$$
>(T^2A, \mu_{TA})\underset{\mu_{A}}{\overset {T_{\alpha}}\rightrightarrows}(TA, \mu_{A})\xtwoheadrightarrow \alpha (A, \alpha)
>$$
>Is a coequalizer diagram in $C^T$.

The theorem is a direct result of applying [[Monadic forgetful functors strictly create coequalizers of Usplit pairs]] with the example in [[Split Coequalizer]].
