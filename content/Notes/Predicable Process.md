# Predicable Process
---
>[!definition]
>Given a probability space $\mathcal{A} = (\Omega, \mathcal F, P)$ with a filtration $\{ \mathcal F_{n} \}$, we call a process $X$ which is adapted to $\{ \mathcal F_{n} \}$ a *predicable process* if 
>$$
>E[\![X_{n+1} | \mathcal F_{n}]\!] = X_{n+1}
>$$

>[!example]
>- All deterministic process are predictable
>- In [[Doob Decomposition Theorem]] The compensation sequence $A$ is a predictable process.
