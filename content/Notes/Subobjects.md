# Subobjects
---
>[!definition]
>A **subobject** of an object $c\in C$ is as [[Monomorphisms and Epimorphisms|monomorphism]] $c'\rightarrowtail c$. Isomorphic subobjects are a pair of subobjects $c'\rightarrowtail c \leftarrowtail c''$ such that there is an isomorphism $c'\cong c''$ which commutes with the subobjects.
>
>The **intersection** of a family of subobjects of $c$ is the limit of the diagram of monomorphism with codomain $c$.

The induced map  from the limit to $c$ is a monomorphism. That means that the legs of the limit cone are also monomorphism, thus the limit is a sub-object of all sub-objects.
