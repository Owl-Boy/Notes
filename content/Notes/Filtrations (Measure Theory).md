# Filtrations
---
>[!tip] Intuition
>Consider a scenario where a fair coin will be tossed 1000 times. You have to figure the expected value of the number of heads in order to say.. guess a gambling strategy.
>With each coin toss, you can choose to update your strategy and at each step you would want to compute the [[Sigma Field]] to let you figure out expected values.

>[!definition]
>Given a measure space $\mathcal{A} = (\Omega, \mathcal F, \mu)$, A filtration on $\mathcal{A}$ is a sequence of increase sub-$\sigma$ fields of $\mathcal F$ denoted as $\{ \mathcal F_{n} \}$
