# Integral Domain
---
Multiplication in general is not as well behaved as it is in numbers like $\mathbb{Z}$ or $\mathbb{R}$, in rings it is possible to have [[Zero Divisors]].

**Integral Domains** are *Rings* which are closer to numbers in the above sense

>[!definition]
>A Ring $R$ is an integral domain if it is a [[Commutative Ring]] and it has no [[Zero Divisors]].

>[!lemma]
>Cancellation of multiplication holds inside Integral Domain.
