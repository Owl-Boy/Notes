# Submodule and quotients
---
A **submodule** $N$ of an $R$-module $M$ is a subgroup of $M$ preserved by the action of $R$. $N$ is also an $R$-module and the inclusion $N \hookrightarrow M$ is a module homomorphism.

>[!example]
>If we view $R$ itself as an $R$-module, using the identity ring homomorphism, then the sub-modules of $R$ are precisely the left-ideals of $R$.

>[!example]
>Both kernel and image of a homormophism $\varphi:M\to M'$ are sub-modules of $M$ and $M'$ respectively.

>[!example]
>If $r$ is in the center of $R$ and $M$ is an $R$-module, then $rM$ is a sub-module of $M$. If $I$ is a left-ideal of $R$ then $IM$ is a submodule of $M$.
