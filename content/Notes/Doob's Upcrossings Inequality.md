# Doob's Upcrossings Inequality
---
>[!theorem]
>Let $X$ be a sub-martingale. Then for $\alpha < \beta$
>$$
>E[U_{m}(\{ X_{j}, 1\leq j \leq m \}, \alpha, \beta)] \leq \frac{E[|X_{m}|+|\alpha|]}{\beta-\alpha}
>$$
