#  Pullbacks in CAT
---
For any functor $F:C \to\text{Set}$ the pullback of the diagram
$$
C\xrightarrow{\quad F\quad}\text{Set}\xleftarrow{\quad U\quad}\text{Set}_{*}
$$
is a category whose objects are pairs $c\in C$ and $(X, x)\in\text{Set}_{*}$. so that $Fc=X$. Morphisms are pairs $f:c\to c'$  and $g:(X,x)\to(X',x')$ so that $Ff=Ug$, which can be expressed as:
