# Homology as generalization of Kernel and Cokernel
---
Consider the following chain complex:
$$
M_{\bullet}:\quad 0\longrightarrow M_{1} \xrightarrow{\;\varphi\;}M_{0}\longrightarrow 0
$$
we get that $H_{1}(M_{\bullet})\cong\text{ker }\varphi$ and $H_{0}(M_{\bullet})\cong\text{coker }\varphi$.
