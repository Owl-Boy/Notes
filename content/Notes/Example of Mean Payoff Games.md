#  Example of Mean Payoff Games
---
![[Pasted image 20251009164605.png]]

Consider a game on the following arena (with starting state $a$). 
- The $\bigcirc$ vertices belong to the maximizer while
- The $\square$ vertices belong to the minimizer.
Some examples of plays:
- $(a,d,e,c)^\omega$ has payoff $\frac{11}{4}$
- $(a,b)^\omega$ has payoff $-2$
- $a,(d,e)^\omega$ has payoff $1$

If both players, play with optimal strategy the play will be the $a,(d,e)^\omega$, thus the payoff of the game will be $1$.
