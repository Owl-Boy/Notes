# Tree Width
---
Informally, the tree-width of a graph is how far away a graph is from being a tree, the lowest tree width is $1$ and the graph with lowest tree width are exactly trees and forests.

There are many ways to define tree-width, one discussed here is width of a [[Tree Decomposition]].

>[!definition]
>The *width* of a tree decomposition of a graph is the size of the largest $X_{i}-1$. The *Tree-width* of a graph is the least possible *width* of any tree-decomposition of the graph.

>[!lemma]
>Given 2 graphs $G, H$, if $H$ is a sub-graph of $G$, the $\text{tw}(H)\leq \text{tw}(G)$.

Any tree decomposition of $G$ is also a tree decomposition of $H$.
