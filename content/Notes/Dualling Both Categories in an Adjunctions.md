# Dualling Both Categories in an Adjunctions
---
Given categories $C$ and $D$ along with the pair of functors $F:C\leftrightarrows D:G$, which form an [[Adjunctions]] such that $F\dashv G$ we can take the [[Duality|dual]] of both the categories and we still get the following:
$$
G^\text{op}:D^\text{op}\leftrightarrows C^\text{op}:F^\text{op}
$$
Here we get that $G^\text{op}\dashv F^\text{op}$.
