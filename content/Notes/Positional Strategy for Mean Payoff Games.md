# Positional Strategy for Mean Payoff Games
---
>[!theorem]
>Given a mean payoff game $(G,w, v_{\text{init}})$
>- There exists a positional strategy $\hat{\sigma}$ for the maximizer such that for any strategy $\tau$ for the minimizer and any startegy $\sigma$ for the maximizer:
>  $$\text{Payoff}(\pi_{\hat{\sigma},\tau})\geq\text{payoff}(\pi_{\sigma,\tau})$$
>- There exists a positional strategy $\hat{\tau}$ for the minimizer such that for any strategy $\sigma$ for the maximizer and $\tau$ for the minimizer:
>  $$\text{Payoff}(\pi_{\sigma,\hat{\tau}})\leq\text{payoff}(\pi_{\sigma,\tau})$$
