# Fundamental Group
---
Given a [[Pointed Type]] $(A, a)$ we have seen that its [[Loop Space]] $\Omega(A,a):\equiv a=a$ has the structure of a group. The only difference being that this type is not necessarily a set, this can be changed by taking the [[Truncation|set truncation]], in general.
>[!definition]
>Given a pointed type $(A, a)$, its **fundamental group** is given by
>$$
>\pi_{1}(A,a):\equiv \|\Omega(A,a)\|_{0}
>$$

And in general the $n^\text{th}$ homotopy group is given by truncation of the $n^\text{th}$ loop space. We also have that the $n^\text{th}$ homotopy group is abelian through the [[Eckmann-Hilton]] argument.
