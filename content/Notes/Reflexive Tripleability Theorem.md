# Reflexive Tripleability Theorem
---
>[!theorem]
>If $U:D\to C$ has a left adjoint and if:
>- $D$ has coequalizers of reflexive pairs
>- $U$ preserves coequalizers of reflexive pairs
>- $U$ reflects isomorphisms.
>
>Then $U$ is [[Monadic Functors|Monadic]].

The theorem can be reduced to [[Monadicity Theorem]]
