# 2-Categories
---
>[!Definition]
> A $2$-category is composed of:
> - Objects, for example categories like $C$
> - $1$-morphisms between pairs of objects
> - $2$-morphisms between parallel pairs of 1 morphisms
> so that:
> - The objects and $1$-morphisms for a category with identities.
> - For any fixed pair of objects $C$ and $D$, the $1$-morphisms $F:C \to D$ and $2$-morphisms between such form a category under the operation called vertical composition.
> - There is also a category whose objects are the Objects and morphisms are pairs of $1$-morphisms with a $2$-morphisms between them, their composition is called horizontal composition.
> - Horizontal composition of Identity natural transformations are identity natural transformations
> - Law of middle 4 interchange holds
