# Pointed Predicates and maps
---
>[!definition]
>- A **pointed predicate** is a predicate over a pointed type $(A,a_{0})$ , it is a family $R:A\to\cal U$ equipped with an element $r_{0}:R(a_{0})$.
>- A **pointed map** between pointed predicate $(R,r_{0})$ and $(S,s_{0})$ is a family of maps $\prod_{a:A}R(a)\to S(a)$ when $g(a_{0},r_{0})=s_{0}$.
