#  P-adic Solenoid
---
Consider the category $\omega^\text{op}$ which is defined as follows:
$$
\cdots \longrightarrow\bullet\longrightarrow\bullet\longrightarrow\bullet\longrightarrow\bullet\longrightarrow\bullet
$$
with $\omega$-many objects.

Now consider the diagram $\omega^\text{op}\to\text{Top}$ where each object goes to a copy of $S_{1}$ and each drawn morphism goes to the covering map $p$ which wraps $p$ times. give by:
$$
\cdots \xrightarrow{\quad\;\quad} S_{1} \xrightarrow{\quad p\quad}S_{1} \xrightarrow{\quad p\quad}S_{1} \xrightarrow{\quad p\quad}S_{1} \xrightarrow{\quad p\quad} S_{1}
$$
The [[Inverse Limits and Direct Limts|Inverse Limit]] of this diagram is called the $P$-adic solenoid.
