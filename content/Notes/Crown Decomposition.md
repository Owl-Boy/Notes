# Crown Decomposition
---
>[!definition]
>A **Crown Decomposition** of a graph $G$ is a partition of the vertex set $V(G)$ into 3 parts, $C,H$ and $R$ such that:
>- $C$ is non-empty.
>- $C$ is an independent set.
>- There are no edges between $C$ and $R$, that is, $H$ separates the other 2 parts
>- Let $E'$ be the set of edges between $C$ and $H$, then there is matching in $E'$ covers $H$.

One can think of $C$ as a crown put on top of the head $H$ and $R$ is the remaining part of the body.
