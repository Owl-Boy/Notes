# Prime Models
---
Let $K$ be a class of $L$-structures. An $L$-structure $\cal M$ is said to be a **prime structure** for $K$ if $\mathcal M \hookrightarrow \mathcal N$ for all $\mathcal N\in K$.

Let $T$ be an $L$-theory. An $L$-structure $\mathcal M$ is an **algebraically prime model** for $T$ if it is both a model for $T$ and a **prime structure** for $\text{Mod}(T)$.

It is called an **Elementary prime model** of $T$ if $\mathcal M \xhookrightarrow{\equiv} \mathcal N$ for all $\mathcal N \models T$.
