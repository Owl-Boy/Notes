# Probability Theory and Gambling
---
>[!faq]
>Why are we taught that gambling is morally bad, while in all probability courses, gambling is the first thing that is used as examples.
>

It cannot be denied that *probability theory* has its roots in gambling.
- [Gerolamo Cardano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerolamo_Cardano) was the first to systematically treat probability theory in his book on gambling.
- [Problem of Points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Problem_of_points), where Pascal and Fermat developed fundamentals of probability theory from a gambling game
- [de Moivre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_de_Moivre) made a living my advising gamblers after he moved to England

But probability theory has many real world use cases from handling covid vaccines to having safe digital signatures.

Most examples in lectures and books use gambling adjacent examples because it simple and precise to state the situation of the arena, motivations of the players etc etc, without requiring getting into the specifics of the subject
