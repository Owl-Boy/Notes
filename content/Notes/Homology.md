# Homology
---
>[!definition]
>The **i-th homology** of a [[Chain Complex]]
>$$
>M_{\bullet} :\quad \cdots \xrightarrow{d_{i+1}} M_{i+1}\xrightarrow{d_{i}} M_{i}\xrightarrow{d_{i-1}} M_{i-1}\xrightarrow{d_{i-2}} \cdots
>$$
>Of $R$-[[Modules]] (Groups) is the $R$-module (group)
>$$
>H_{i}(M_{\bullet}) := \frac{\text{ker }d_{i}}{\text{im }d_{i+1}}
>$$

We call the complex [[Exact Sequences|exact]] at a module (group) if its homotopy at the module (group) is $0$.
