# Pushouts in CAT
---
Consider the diagram:
![[Pasted image 20250609171116.png]]

$P$ is the colimit cone under the diagram $\mathbb{1}\longleftarrow \mathbb {1}\sqcup\mathbb 1 \longrightarrow \mathbb 2$. And a functor $P\to C$ is a cone under the given diagram with nadir $C$.

A cone under such a diagram picks out an endomorphism in $C$. Hence $P$ cannot have any relations on the endomorphism picked in it. This $P$ is the category representing the monoid $\mathbb{N}$ under addition.

To get $\mathbb{Z}$ we replace $\mathbb 2$ with the walking isomorphism $\mathbb I$.
