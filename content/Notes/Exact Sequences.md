# Exact Sequences
---
Consider a chain complex $(M_{\bullet},d_{\bullet})$, The complex is said to be **exact** at a module $M_{i}$ if the image of $d_{i+1}$ completely overlaps the kernel of $d_{i}$. Another helpful diagram from Chapter 0:
![[Pasted image 20250703151347.png]]

>[!definition]
>A complex is called an **Exact Sequence** if it is exact at all modules in it.
