# Stochastic Languages
---
>[!note]
>*Stochastic Languages* are just [[Threshold Languages]] for [[Probabilistic Automata]].

>[!definition]
>A *Stochastic Language* $\mathcal{L}$ is a language such that there exists a [[Probabilistic Automata]] $\mathcal{A}$ for it such that:
>$$
>\mathcal{L} = \{ w | A(w) \bowtie \theta \}
>$$
>for some $\theta \in [0,1]$ and some $\bowtie \in \{ <, \leq, \neq, =,\geq, >  \}$
