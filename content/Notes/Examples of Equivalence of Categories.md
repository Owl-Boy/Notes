# Examples of Equivalence of Categories
---
>[!example]
>For any field ${\mathbb k}$, the category $\text{Mat}_{\mathbb k}$ and $\text{Vect}_{\mathbb k}^\text{fd}$ are equivalent.
>We can build the following functors which are all equivalences:
>$$
>\text{Mat}_{\mathbb k} \underset{H}{\overset{\mathbb k^{(-)}}{\leftrightarrows}} \text{Vect}_{\mathbb k}^\text{basis} \underset{C}{\overset{U}{\leftrightarrows}}\text{Vect}_{\mathbb k}^\text{fd}
>$$ 

>[!example]
>Any connected groupoids is equivalent, as a category, to the automorphism group of any of its objects. 

Consider the automorphism group of any object $G(g, g)$ of the groupoid and consider the inclusions map. This is full and faithful by definition, and essentially surjective because the groupoids is connected.

>[!Example] Corollary of the previous example
>In a path connected space $X$, any choice of basepoints $x\in X$ yeilds and isomorphic fundamental group.
