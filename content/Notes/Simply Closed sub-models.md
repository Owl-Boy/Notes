# Simply Closed sub-models
---
> [!DEF]
> Given models $\mathcal M, \mathcal N\models T$ with $M\subseteq N$. We called $\mathcal M$ to be simply closed if for all quantifier free $\phi(\underline v, w)$ and $\underline a\in \mathcal M$ we get
> $$
> \mathcal N \models \exists w.\phi(\underline a, w)\quad\text{ iff }\quad \mathcal M \models \exists w.\phi(\underline a, w)
> $$
