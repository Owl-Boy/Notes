# Embeddings reflect n-Types
---
>[!theorem]
>If $f:X\to Y$ is an [[Surjections and Embeddings|embedding]] and $Y$ is an $n$-type, then so is $X$.

Let $x,x':X$, then we show that $x=x'$ is an $n-1$-type, but since $f$ is an embedding we have $(x=x')\simeq(f(x)=f(x'))$ and the latter is an $n-1$-type by assumption.
