# U-split Coequalizer
---
>[!definition]
>Given a functor $U:D\to C$
>- A $U$**-split coequalizer** is a parallel pair $f, g:x\rightrightarrows y$ in $D$ togehter  with an extension of the pair $Uf, Ug$ to a split coequalier in $C$.
>- $U$ **creates coequzliers of $U$-split pairs** if any $U$-split coequalizer admits a coequalizer in $D$ whose image under $U$ is isomorphic to the fork underlying the given $U$-split coequalizer diagram in $C$, and if any such fork in $D$ is a coequalizer.
>- $U$ **strictly creates coequalizer of $U$-split pairs** if any $U$-split coequalizer admits a unique lift to a coequalizer in $D$ for the given parallel pair.
