# Retract of an Equivalence is an Equivalence
---
>[!lemma]
>If $g$ is a retract of and equivalence $f$, then $g$ is also an equivalence.

Since by [[Retracts (HoTT)#^447d61]], every fiber of $g$ is a retract of a fiber of $f$. And since all fibers of $f$ are contractibe, so are all fibers of $g$.
