# Elementary Equivalence of Models
---
Two structures $M$ and $N$ are said to be elementarily equivalent iff they have the same theory.

$N$ is called an **Elementary embedding** or an **elementary substructure** if $N$ is an embedding/ substructure of $M$ and it is elementarily equivalent to $M$.

The [[Tarski-Vaught test]] gives a necessary and sufficient condition to check if given a substructure, it is elementary.
