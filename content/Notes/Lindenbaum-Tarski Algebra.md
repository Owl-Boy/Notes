# Lindenbaum-Tarski Algebra
---
The **Lindenbaum-Tarski Algebra** is a way of *"Algebraizing Logic"*. The idea is to identify logical statements that are logically equivalent.

Different Kinds of Logic lead to different kinds of **Lindenbaum-Tarski Algebra**, for example:
- [[Boolean Algebra]] for *Classical Logic*
- [[Heyting Algebra]] for [[Intuitionistic Logic]]
- [[Frames(Algebra)]] for [[Geometric Propositional Logic]]
