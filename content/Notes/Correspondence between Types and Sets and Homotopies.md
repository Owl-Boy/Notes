#  Correspondence between Types and Sets and Homotopies
---
$$
\begin{matrix}
\hline 
\text{Types} & \text{Logic} & \text{Sets} & \text{Homotopy} \\
\hline  
A & \text{proposition} & \text{set}  & \text{space} \\
a:A & \text{proof} & \text{element} & \text{point} \\
B(x) & \text{predicate} & \text{family of sets} & \text{fibration} \\
b(x):B(x) & \text{conditional proof} & \text{family of elements} & \text{section} \\
\mathbf{0,1} & \top,\bot & \emptyset,\{ \emptyset \} & \emptyset, * \\
A+B & A \lor B & \text{disjoint union} & \text{coproduct} \\
A \times B & A \land B & \text{cartesian product} & \text{product} \\
A \to B & A \Rightarrow B & \text{set of functions} & \text{function space} \\
\sum_{(x:A)}B(x) & \exists_{(x:A)}B(x) & \text{disjoint sum} & \text{ total space} \\
\prod_{(x:A)} B(x)  &  \forall_{(x:A)} B(x) & \text{product} & \text{space of sections} \\
\text{Id}_{A} & \text{equality} & f: x \mapsto x & \text{path space } A^I 
\end{matrix}
$$
