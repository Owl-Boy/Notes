# Free T-Algebra
---
>[!definition]
>A **Free $T$-Algebra** is an object
>$$
>F^Tc:=(Tc,\mu_{c}:T^2C\to Tc)
>$$
>in the category of algebras that is in the image of the free functor $F^T:C \to C^T$
