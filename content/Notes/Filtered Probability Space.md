# Filtered Probability Space
---
A *filtered probability space* is a [[Measure Space|Probability Space]] with a [[Filtrations (Measure Theory)|Filtration]] associated with it.

>[!definition]
>A *Filtered Probability Space* is a 4-tuple defined as:
>$$
>\mathcal A = \langle \Omega, \mathcal F, \{ \mathcal F_{n} \}, P \rangle
>$$
