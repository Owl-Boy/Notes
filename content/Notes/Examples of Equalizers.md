# Examples of Equalizers
---
>[!example] Sets
>Consider 2 set $A, B$ and a pair of parallel functions $f, g:A \to B$, then their equalizer, would be isomorphic to the set 
>$$C = \{ a\in A \mid f(a) = g(a) \}$$

>[!example] Kernel
>Consider a group homomorphism $\phi: G \to H$, and consider the trivial homomorphism $e:G \to H$ which sends the entire group to the identity element, the equalizer for this pair of maps is the subgroup $K$ of $G$ along with the inclusions map, where $K$ is the kernel of $\phi$.
>
>Similarly, for maps $\phi, \psi$, their equalizer is the kernel of the map $\phi-\psi$.
