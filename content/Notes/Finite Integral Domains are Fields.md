# Finite Integral Domains are Fields
---
>[!theorem]
>Assume $R$ is a finite commutative ring. Then $R$ is an [[Integral Domain]] iff it is a [[Fields|Field]].

Let $a$ be a non-zero divisor of a finite cring $R$. That means multiplication by $a$ is surjective. And any surjection endofunction of a finite set is a bijection, hence it must send an element to $1$. That means $a$ is a unit.

In an integral domain, all non-zero elements are non-zero divisors, hence are units.
