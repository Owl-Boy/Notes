# Constructing the Free Group
---
Consider the forgetful functor $U:\text{Group}\to\text{Set}$ and a set $S$. Let $\Phi'$ be the set of representatives for isomorphism classes of groups that can be generated by a set of elements of cardinality at most $|S|$. Let $\Phi$ be the set of function $S\to UG$ with $G\in \Phi'$ such that the image of the function generates $G$. This is a jointly weakly initial set of objects in the comma category of $S\downarrow U$, and a solution set for $U$.

To construct the initial object, which by [[A functor admits a left adjoint iff all its comma categories have an initial object]] defines the free group on $S$, from the product, indexed by the set $\Phi$, of the groups appearing as codomains and consider the induced map.
$$
S\xrightarrow{\hat{\eta}} U\left( \prod_{S\to UG\in \Phi}G \right).
$$
The map $\hat{\eta}$ is the product of the objects of $\Phi$ in the category $S\downarrow U$. Let $\Gamma$ be the subgroup of the product generated by the image of $\hat{\eta}$. We show that $\Gamma$ is the gree group on $S$ by proving that the codomain restrictoin $\eta:S\to U\Gamma$ is initial in $S\downarrow U$.

Given a group $H$ and a function $S\to UH$, let $G\subseteq H$ be the subgroup generated by the image of $S$. By construction, $\psi$ factors through its codomains restriction $\phi:S\to UG$, and since $G$ is generated by the image of the map $\phi\in \Phi$. The map $\phi$equal the composite of $\eta:S\to U \Gamma$ with the projection component indexed by $\phi$. so we have found a way to factor $\psi$ through $\eta$ via a homomorphism $\Gamma \to H$. The uniqueness of this factorization follows from the fact that $\Gamma$ is generated by the image of $S$; as $\psi:S\to UH$ specifies the images of these generators no alternate choices are possible.
