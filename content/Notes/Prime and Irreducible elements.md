# Prime and Irreducible elements
---
>[!definition]
>- An element $a\in R$ is called **prime** if the ideal $(a)$ is [[Prime Ideals and Maximal Ideals|prime]].
>  $$
>  a|bc \implies a|b\text{ or } a|c
>  $$
>- An element $a$ is **irreducible** if $a$ is not a unit and
>  $$a = bc \implies (b\text{ is a unit or}c\text{ is a unit.})
>  $$

Some ways to think about **irreducible** elements:
- $a=bc$ implies $a$ is an associate of $b$ or $c$.
- $a=bc$ implies $(a)=(b)$ or $(a)=(c)$.
- $(a) \subseteq (b)$ implies $(a)=(b)$ or $(b)=(1)$
- $(a)$ is maximal among proper [[Principle Ideals]].
