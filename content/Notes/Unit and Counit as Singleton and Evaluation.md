# Unit and Counit as Singleton and Evaluation
---
Consider the adjunction $F \dashv U$ where $U:\text{Group}\to\text{Set}$ is the forgetful functor and $F:\text{Set}\to\text{Group}$ is the free-functor.

Here the components of the unit adjunctions $\eta:S \mapsto UFS$ takes a set $S$ to the set of words generated by $S$. The map takes and element $s:S$ to the singleton word $[s]$.

The components of the counit are the group homomorphism $\epsilon_{G}:FUG \to G$ that sends the group of words where elements of $G$ are thought of as the alphabet to the group $G$. Here we are forced to send the singleton to its value, hence the homomorphism just becomes the evaluation map.
