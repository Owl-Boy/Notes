# Polynomial Rings over a Noetherian Ring are Noetherian
---
>[!theorem]
>Let $R$ be a [[Noetherian Ring and PIDs|Noetherian Ring]] then $R[x_{1},x_{2},x_{3}\dots x_{n}]$ is [[Noetherian Ring and PIDs|noetherian]].

This is a direct consequence of [[Hilbert's Basis Theorem]].

This is interesting because [[Finite Type Algebra]]s are very far from finitely generated [[Modules]] over $R$ so you would not expect them to be [[Noetherian Modules]] over $R$. But a lot of them are [[Noetherian Ring and PIDs|Noetherian Rings]].
