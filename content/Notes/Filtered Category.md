# Filtered Category
---
>[!definition]
>A category $J$ is **Filtered** if there is a cocone under every finite diagram in $J$.

>[!example]
>Any ordinal category is **filtered**.

>[!example]
>The parallel pair category, and discrete categories with more than 1 objects are not **filtered**.
