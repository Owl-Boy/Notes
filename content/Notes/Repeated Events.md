# Repeated Events and Partial Control
---
Analysis of a big bunch of elements is hard. To do so, assumptions like rvs being Identical, or being independent with some control on variation etc etc have been considered. [[Martingales]] are a generalization of this idea.

Another extension to this idea is that of there being asymmetric control over the game in different parties. For example, 
- One can repay the entire loan at any time with whatever interest they have at the moment and they don't have to pay the amount they would have if they waited the entire duration. The bank must play the way we want it to.
- A gambling house would have a sequence of games that a player can play, and they can quit whenever they feel like they would loose money. The house must play all games.

These also indicate that things must be re-evaluated in a very different manner in such scenarios
