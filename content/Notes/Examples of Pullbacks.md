# Examples of Pullbacks
---
>[!example]
>Consider the quotient map $\rho:\mathbb{R} \to S^1$ that sends the number $t$ to the point $e^{2\pi it}$ of $S^1$. The pull back of the diagram:
>![[Pasted image 20250516160548.png|100]]
>is the fiber over a point of $S_{1}$, hence is the discrete subspace $\mathbb{Z}$.

>[!Example]
>Consider the maps from $\mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}$ in the category of abelian groups. These correspond to the element $n$ which the the output of $1$ according to any homomorphism. The legs of the pullback
>![[Pasted image 20250516162309.png|120]]
>are elements $a$ and $b$ such that $am = bn$.
