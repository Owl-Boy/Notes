# Concrete Categories
---
>[!definition] 
>A **concrete category** is a category $C$ equipped with a faithful functor $U:C \to \text{Set}$.

This typically carries an object of $C$ to its underlying set. The faithfulness condition asserts that any pair of parallel morphisms that are identical on the underlying sets, are indentical.

These are used in the situation when it is easier to show that a diagram commutes by showing that for the underlying sets the functions commute.
