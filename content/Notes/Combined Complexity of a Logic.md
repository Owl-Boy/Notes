# Combined Complexity of a Logic
---
**Combined Complexity** combines [[Data Complexity of a Logic|Data Complexity]] and [[Expression Complexity of a Logic|Expression Complexity]] for the model checking problem:

>[!definition] 
>The combined complexity of a logic $\mathcal{L}$ is $\mathcal K$ if the language
>$$
>\{ (\text{enc}(\mathfrak A), \text{enc}(\Phi))\; | \; \mathfrak A \vDash \Phi \}
>$$
>belongs to $\mathcal K$.
