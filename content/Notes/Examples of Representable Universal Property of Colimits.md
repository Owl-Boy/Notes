#  Examples of Representable Universal Property of Colimits
---
>[!example]
>In any locally small category $C$ for any triple of objects $X,Y$ and $Z$, the representable property of coproducts looks like:
>$$
>C(X\sqcup Y, Z) \cong C(X, Z)\times C(Y,Z)
>$$

>[!example]
>Iterated co-product of an object with itself is called a *co-power* or *tensor*. For a set $I$, the $I$-indexed copower of $A$, denoted as $\coprod_{i\in I}A_{i}$ or $I\cdot A$. The representable property is expressed by the natural isomorphism:
>$$
>C(\coprod_{I}A, X) \cong C(A, X)^I
>$$
