# Noetherian Modules
---
>[!definition]
>A module $M$ over a ring $R$ is called **Noetherian** if every finite module of $M$ is [[Free Module|finitely generated]] as an $R$-module.

Thus a ring $R$ is called [[Noetherian Ring and PIDs|Neotherian]] iff it is a **Noetherian Module** over itself.
