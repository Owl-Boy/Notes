#  Tree Decomposition of k-connected Graphs
---
>[!lemma]
>A $k$-connected graph has [[Tree Width]] at least $k$.

***FTSOC*** Assume that there is a Tree decomposition with width $k-1$.

Then consider 2 adjacent vertices of the tree and use the [[Tree Decomposition Lemma 1]] to show that if one removes vertices in the intersection of the bags of the vertices, the graph gets disconnected. But that shows that the graph is at most $k-1$ connected which is a contradiction.
