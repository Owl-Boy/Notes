# Finitely Satisfiable and Valid Sentences
---
>[!definition]
>Given a vocabulary $\sigma$, a sentence $\Phi$ in that vocabulary is called **Finitely Satisfiable** if there a finite structure $\mathcal{A}$ such that $\mathcal{A} \vDash \Phi$.
>A sentence $\Phi$ is called **Finitely Valid** for all finite structures $\mathcal{A}\in \text{STRUCT}[\sigma]$, we have $\mathcal{A} \vDash \Phi$
