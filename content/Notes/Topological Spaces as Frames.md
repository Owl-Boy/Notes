# Topological Spaces as Frames
---
Let $X$ bet a set, and let $A$ be any subframe of $\mathcal P(A)$. i.e Any family of subsets of $X$ that:
- Contain the empty set and $X$
- Are closed under arbitrary union
- Are closed under finite intersection

This exactly happens to be the definition of a [[Topological Spaces|Topology]], where the elements of $A$ happen to be the open subsets of $X$.
