# Locality
---
Winning games is non-trivial even for fairly simple examples, but often, one can avoid complicated combinatorial arguments, by using rather simple sufficient conditions that guarantee a winning strategy for the duplicator. 

For [[First Order Logic]], a lot of these conditions are based on the idea that first order logic can only analyse the structure up to a finite length from the assignments of the free variables, hence a lot of global properties like cardinality, connectedness and so on are not definable in FO.

These ideas are formally captured by the concept of **Locality**.

The two common notions of locality that will be discussed are:
- [[Hanf-Locality]]
- [[Gaifman-Locality]]
