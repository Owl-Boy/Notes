# The Category Ring
---
The [[Category]] of rings, called $\text{Ring}$ is defined with [[Ring|Rings]] as its set of objects and [[Ring Homomorphisms]] as its morphisms.

This category has a terminal element, which is the *Zero ring*, and the ring $\mathbb{Z}$ is the initial object.
