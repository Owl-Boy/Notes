# Examples of Modules
---
>[!example]
>Every abelian group is is a module over the ring $\mathbb{Z}$.
>That too in exactly 1 way as there is only one morphism from $\mathbb{Z}$ to the the endomorphism ring of the group as $\mathbb{Z}$ is the intial ring.

>[!Example]
>If $R$ is a field $k$, then we call the module a *Vector space* over the field $k$.

>[!example]
>Given any homomorphism of rings $\alpha : R \to S$, one can define a module with  $\rho: R \times S \to S$ such that $\rho(r, s)=\alpha(r) \cdot s$. People usually just write this as $rs$ instead of $\alpha(r)s$.

^0b44ae

>[!example]
>In the [[Examples of Modules#^0b44ae|previous example]], if $R$ is a commutative ring, and $\alpha$ maps $R$ to the center of $S$ then it would be called an $R$-algebra.
