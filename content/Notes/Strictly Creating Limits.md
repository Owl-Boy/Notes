# Strictly Creating Limits
---
>[!definition]
>A functor $F:C\to D$ **strictly creates** limits for a given class of diagrams if for any $K:J\to C$ in the class, the limit cone over $FK : J \to D$:
>- There is a unique lift of the cone to the cone over $K$ 
>- This lift defines a limit cone over $K$.
