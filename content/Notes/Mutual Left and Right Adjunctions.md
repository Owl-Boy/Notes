# Mutual Left and Right Adjunctions
---
Recall the idea of taking [[Duality|duals]] of categories in [[Dualling Both Categories in an Adjunctions]]. If we do that for only one of the categories then we get the following:

>[!definition]
>Given a pair of contravariant functors $F:C^\text{op}\to D$ and $G:D^\text{op}\to C$ are **mutually left adjoint** if there exists a natural isomorphism
>$$
>D(Fc, d) \cong C(Gd, c)
>$$
>or **mutually right adjoint** if there exists a natural isomorphism
>$$
>D(d, Fc) \cong C(c, Gd).
>$$

These are sometimes called [[Antitone Galois Connections]].

Similar to the definition [[Unit and Counit as Universal Arrows]] a **mutual left adjunction** is equipped with a pair of Counits and a **mutual right adjunction** is equipped with a pair of units.
