# Examples of Rings
---
>[!example]
>One can define a ring structure on the trivial group $\{ \star \}$, this is often called the **Zero Ring**.

>[!Example]
>Most kinds of numbers, like $\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{C}$ are all rings.

>[!Example]
>Fixing a field $\cal F$, the set of all $n \times n$ matrices over $\cal F$ form a ring, where addition is component-wise, and multiplication is the multiplication of matrices.

>[!example]
>For any $n$, we have that $\mathbb{Z} / n\mathbb{Z}$ form a ring.
