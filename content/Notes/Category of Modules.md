# Category of Modules
---
The category of modules over a [[Ring]] $R$, usually denoted as $R\!-\!\text{Mod}$ has [[Modules|R-Modules]] as its objects, and *module homomorphisms* as its morphism.

The $\mathbf{0}$-mod is a [[Initial, Terminal and Zero Objects|zero object]] in the category $R$-mod.

This category can be thought of as the category of abelian groups enriched with the ring-structure $R$. 

Also, one difference form the category of abelian groups:
$$
\text{kernel} \iff\text{submodule}
$$
or, every monomorphism is a sub-module.
