# Smooth Manifold
---
Let $X$ be a [[Topological Manifold]].

>[!theorem]
>An **Atlas** for a [[Topological Manifold]] $X$ is **smooth** iff for any two charts, the [[Transition Functions|transition function]] is smooth.
