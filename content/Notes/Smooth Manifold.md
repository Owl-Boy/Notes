# Smooth Manifold
---
Let $X$ be a [[Topological Manifold]].

>[!definition]
>Let $\cal A$ be a smooth atlas on $X$. And Let $(U, f)$ be a coordinate chart on $X$. $(U, f)$ is said to be **compatible** with $\cal A$. Iff the transition function between $(U, f)$ and any coordinate chart in the atlas is smooth.
>
>Let $\cal A$ and $\cal B$ two smooth atlases for $X$. Then $A$ and $B$ are said to be **compatible** iff every chart in $A$ is compatible with every chart in $B$.


>[!lemma]
>compatibility between 2 smooth atlases is an equivalence relation.

>[!definition]
>A **smooth manifold** is a [[Topological Manifold]] $X$ together with an equivalence class $\cal A$ of compatible smooth atlases on $X$. The equivalence class of smooth atlases is called a **smooth structure** on $X$.
