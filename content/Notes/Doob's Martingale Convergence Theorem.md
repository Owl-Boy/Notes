# Doob's Martingale Convergence Theorem
---
*Doob's Martingale Convergence Theorem* talks about limits of supermartingales, this typically refers to the theorem that if a sequence of supermartinagles satisfy some bounds must converge.

This is the martingale version of the [[Monotone Convergence Theorem]].

>[!theorem]
>Let $\{ X_{n} \}$ be a sequence of Martingales which are uniformly integrable, i.e
>$$
>\sup_{n} E[\![|X_{n}|]\!] = K < \infty
>$$
>Then there exists an integrable random variable $\xi$ such that $\{ X_{n} \}$ converges to $\xi$ in $L^1(P)$.
>Further, if 
>$$
>\sup_{n} E[\![|X_{n}^p|]\!] = K' < \infty
>$$
>Then the sequence converges to $\xi$ in $L^p(P)$
