# Free Algebra
---
Just like [[Free Module]], a **Free Algebra** over a given set $A$ is the Algebra that is defined by adding the lest possible algebra structure on $A$.

>[!theorem]
>$R[A]$, which is the polynomial ring over the commutative ring $R$ is the **Free $R$-Algebra** over the set $A$.

Given a set map $\varphi:A \to S$, where $S$ is an $R$-algebra. Then mapping the element $A$ in $R[A]$ as described by the above map is the single unique way to factorise $\varphi$.
