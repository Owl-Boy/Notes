# ACFp is $\kappa$ categorical for uncountable $\kappa$
---

The cardinality of the an algebraically closed field is $\lambda +\aleph_0$ where $\lambda$ is the transcendence degree. Therefore if algebraically closed field has an infinite cardinality $\kappa$ iff its transcendence degree is $\alpha$
