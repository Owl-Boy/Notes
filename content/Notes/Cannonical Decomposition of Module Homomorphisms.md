# Cannonical Decomposition of Module Homomorphisms
---
>[!theorem]
>Every $R$-module homomorphism $\varphi:M \to M'$ can be decomposed as follows:
>$$
>M \twoheadrightarrow M /\text{ker}\ \varphi \underset{\tilde{\varphi}}{\xrightarrow{\sim}} \text{im}\ \varphi \hookrightarrow M'
>$$
>Where the isomorphism $\tilde{\varphi}$ is induced by varphi.

This is analogous to the *First Isomorphism Theorem for Groups*.
