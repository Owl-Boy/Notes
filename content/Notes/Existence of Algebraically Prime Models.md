# Existence of Algebraically Prime Models
---
> [!THM]
> A Theory $T$ has [[Prime Models|Algebraically Prime Models]] if for all $\mathcal A \models (T_\forall)^\models$, there exists a model $M$ of $T$ and an embedding $A\xhookrightarrow{i}\mathcal M$ such that all embedding $A\xhookrightarrow {j} \mathcal N$ factor through $i$.

The proof seems lowkey trivial.

Such a model $\mathcal M$ will be an algebarically prime model as given any other model $\mathcal N$, there exists a sub-model of $\mathcal N$ that staifies $T_\forall$ and that would give an embedding from $M$ to $N$.
