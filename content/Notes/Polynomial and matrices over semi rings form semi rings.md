# Polynomial and matrices over semi rings form semi rings
---
Given a semi ring $\mathcal S$, the following are also semi rings:
- Polynomials over $\mathcal S$
- For all $n$, all $n \times n$ matrices over $\cal S$

The proof is easy and the identity elements are what one would expect:
For polynomials the identity element is $1$ and the anihilator is the $0$ element of the semi ring.

For matrices of order $n \times n$ the anihilator is the all $0$ matrix and the identity is the matrix with $1$ on the diagonal and $0$ everywhere else.
