# Spectrums in PIDs
---
In a [[Noetherian Ring and PIDs|PID]], since [[Prime Ideals are Maximal in PIDs]], the partial orderings of the prime ideals look all the same.;
- There is the $(0)$ ideal at the bottom
- All other prime ideals are on the level above it, incomparable with one another.

>[!example]
>Consider the field $\mathbb{C}$, hence the PID $\mathbb{C}[x]$, turns our that since $\mathbb{C}$ is an [[Algebraic Closure|Algebraically Closed Field]] we get that the PIDs in $\mathbb{C}[x]$ look like $(x-z)$, for any $z$ in $\mathbb{C}$, hence the spectrum looks as follows:

>[!lemma]
>For this reason, in a PID, the [[Krull Dimension]] of the ring is always $1$.
