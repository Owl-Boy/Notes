# Homotopy Group
---
Using [[Loop Space|loop spaces]], the **homotopy group** of a space is pretty easy to define:

>[!definition]
>Given $n\geq {1}$ and $(A, a)$ which is a pointed space, the homotopy group of $A$ at $a$ is defined as:
>$$
>\pi_{n}(A, a) :\equiv \| \Omega^n(A, a)\|_{0}
>$$

We also have by [[Eckmann-Hilton]] that for $n\geq {2}$ the homotopy groups are [[Abelian Groups|abelian]].
