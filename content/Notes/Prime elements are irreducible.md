# Prime elements are irreducible
---
>[!theorem]
>Let $R$ be an [[Integral Domain]], and $a\in R$ be a non-zero [[Prime elements are irreducible|prime]], then $a$ is also [[Prime and Irreducible elements|irreducible]].

Since $(a)$ is not a prime we have $(a)\neq (1)$ and let $a=bc$. Consider $(b)$ and $(c)$, we have that $a\in(b)$ and $a\in (c)$ so $(a) \subseteq (b)$ and $(a)\subseteq(c)$. But since $(a)$ is a maximal principle idea we have $(a)=(b)$ or $(a)=(c)$, so $a$ is an associate of one of the two.
