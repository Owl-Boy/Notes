# Reflective Subcategory
---
>[!definition]
>A **reflective subcategory** of a category $C$ is a full subcategory $D$ so that the inclusion admits a left adjoint called the **reflector** or **localization**:
>![[Pasted image 20250714170058.png|150]]

We can now think the components of the unit have the form $c\to Lc$, the mnemonic is that an object looks like its reflection. The following implies tha the components of the counit $Ld\cong d$ form an isomorphism. Via the counit, any object in a reflective subcategory is naturally isomorphic to its reflection back into the subcategory.
