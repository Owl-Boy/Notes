# Unit Circle is a Manifold
---
The circle $S^1$ is a $1$-dimensional manifold.

We define $S^1$ as:
$$
S^1 = \{ (x, y)\mid x^2 +y^2 = 1\} \subseteq R^2
$$
equipped with subspace topology.

We will now build the coordinate charts, we use steriographic projection for both of them, first from the north pole and then from the south pole.

A similar construction works for spheres of higher dimension.
