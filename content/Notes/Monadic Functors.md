# Monadic Functors
---

>[!definition]
>An Adjunction $F\dashv U$ is called **monadic** if the canonical comparison functor from [[Kleisli Category and Eilenberg Moore Category in the Category of Adjunctions]] from $D$ to the [[Eilenberg Moore Category]] of the induced monad on $C$ defines an equivalence of categories.
>
>A functor $U:D\to C$ is **monadic** if it admits a left adjoint that defines a monadic [[Adjunctions|adjunction]].
