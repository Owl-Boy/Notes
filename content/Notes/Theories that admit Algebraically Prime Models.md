#  Theories that admit Algebraically Prime Models
---
Both proofs use the theorem [[Existence of Algebraically Prime Models]].

> [!EXAMPLE]
> The Theory of Divisible Acyclic Groups. The $\forall$-theory for DAG is the theory of torsion free abelian groups. Given an torsion free abelian group, do a construction that is like the [[Fraction Field]].

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Consider the theory of algebraically closed fields. Its $\forall$ theory is theory of [[Integral Domain]]s. Thus, given any integral domain, we do a fraction field construction and then take the closure.
