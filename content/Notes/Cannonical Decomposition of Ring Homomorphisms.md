# Cannonical Decomposition of Ring Homomorphisms
---
>[!theorem]
>Every ring homomorphism $\phi: R \to S$ can be decomposed as follows:
>$$
>R \twoheadrightarrow R /\text{ker}(\phi) \underset{\tilde{\phi}}{\xrightarrow{\sim}} \text{im} \phi \hookrightarrow S
>$$

This is analogous to the *First Isomorphism Theorem for Groups*.
