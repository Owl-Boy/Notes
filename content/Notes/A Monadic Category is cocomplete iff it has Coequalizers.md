# A Monadic Category is cocomplete iff it has Coequalizers
---
>[!lemma]
>Given a category $C$ which is cocomplete and let $U:A\to C$ be monadic. Then the following statements are equivalent:
>- $A$ is cocomplete.
>- $A$ has coequalizers

Forward direction is trivial. By [[Any Category with Coproducts and Coequalizers is Cocomplete, with Products and Equalizers is Complete]] we now only need to show that $A$ has coproducts.
