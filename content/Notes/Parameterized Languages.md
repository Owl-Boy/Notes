# Parameterized Languages
---
>[!definition]
>Given a finite set of alphabet $\Sigma$, a **parameterized language** $P$ over $\Sigma$ is a collection of words over $\Sigma$ with a natural number tag.
>$$
>P \subseteq \Sigma^* \times \mathbb{N}
>$$
